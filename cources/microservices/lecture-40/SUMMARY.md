## Lecture 40. Vertical Slice Architecture vs Clean Architecture

Here are the key differences between Vertical Slice Architecture vs Clean Architecture:
- VSA emphasizes organizing software development around features, cutting through all layers, while CA focuses on separation of concerns and dependency rules. It organizes code into layers.
- VSA development teams concentrate on delivering complete features each potentially touching all layers of the app. CA more structured approach, ensuring that business logic is decoupled from external concerns.
- VSA well-suited for agile teams working on complex applications with numerous features.
- CA ideal for large-scale applications where long-term maintenance, scalability and the ability to adapt changes business requirements.

When to chose VSA and when CA?
- When features should be delivered rapidly and independently - VSA is a better choice.
- For team practicing Agile or Scrum that needs to deliver complete features in short cycles.

SUMMARY: VSA for faster development, CA - for better scalability.
